You swing open the door and a man stands the opposite side of the room, he is
just covered by shadow enough for him to remain faceless. Startled, you trip
backwards, narrowly avoiding falling by catching yourself on the doorframe.
"Oh thank god I-" you stop. The mans arm is held out infront of him, something
sits in his palm. He's too still. A mannequin! you breathe a sigh of relief and
take a step forward into the room. The same plastic chairs and tables from the
cafe have also been placed in this room - talk about keeping a budget. You
approach the figure at the back of the room, taking a loop around it. In this
moment you realise... the quiet isn't pure anymore. It's being tainted by short
breaths, excited hiccups. It is coming from the mannequin.
