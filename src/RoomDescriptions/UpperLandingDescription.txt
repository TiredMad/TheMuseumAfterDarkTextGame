You stand with your back to the top step of the great staircase as you take in
the Upper Landing around you. You turned around once but looking down to the
floor below gave you a stomach turning sense of vertigo.  This isn't usually
something you experience, but you chalk it up to the headache still plaguing
you. A distant thump pulls your eyes to the left. The noise reminds you of
just how quiet the building is. It's not the quiet per se, but the total lack
of sound all together. No matter how quiet an environment a sound is always
present. This could be your own heartbeat, the sound of blood rushing past
your eardrums, the rustling of your clothes - a constant following you your
whole life - and yet... you hear nothing. This makes the mysterious thump
almost... alluring. The sound appears to have come from the museum's staff
room, just to your left. 

A tight corridor opens up in the wall just ahead of the staircase. At its
shadowy end an unassuming green door sits. A metal sign bolted just below its
porthole window states "Guard Room - Staff Only." You approach the door and
knock firmly twice. No answer. You knock again, in a slightly more frantic
manner this time, but the result remains the same. This door has a handle, a
fact that you never expected could bring you joy. Said joy quickly fades when
the handle refuses to shift. This door has a key card lock....
