You approach the fire door and push it open. The door protests on its hinges,
scraping along the floor as you force it ahead. The door stops short in its
path, forcing you to squeeze through the small gap you are afforded. As you
emerge out of the other side you are greeted by the museum's main hall.
Directly infront of you a staircase unfolds. Grand stone pillars stand either
side of its beginning, arching off into bespoke banisters that climb towards
the Edwardian ceilings. To your left, you see the sign for "Dinosaur Gallery"
the familiar dinosaur exhibit you've taken your niece Mila to time after time.
To your right, the Gift Shop in which you have spent a small fortune in over
the years, purchasing various toys.
