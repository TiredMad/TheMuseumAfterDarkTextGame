You feel beneath you a hard floor. As you take a breath the lingering smell of
stale coffee fills your nose. Your eyes flutter open. With your cheek pressed
against the cold linoleum, you take in the sight of plastic chairs outlining
cheaply made tables populating the large room you find yourself in. Half-eaten
meals dirty the plates upon the surfaces around you. You peel yourself to your
knees. The strong ache in your head concerns you slightly but your whereabouts
are a more pressing matter in your mind. This new angle affords you a view of
a hot counter situated on the north wall, off to its left a large heavy fire
door looms. To your right, a brochure holder informs you that you are
currently in the Notlaw Museum. You don't recall it having a cafe, and yet
here you are. To your left, a large decorative clock informs you that it is
currently 3am. Your dog is probably waiting at the front door for his evening
meal. You decide that you should make your way home...
