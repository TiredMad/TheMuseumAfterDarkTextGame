The crimson velvet curtains that guard Notlaw Museums Dinosaur exhibit breathe
forward into the main hall, causing a cold draught to hit your bare arms. As
you brush them aside and enter the long corridor that houses the gallery, a
shiver crawls its way down your spine as you notice that all the windows are
closed. You could swear you hear footsteps trailing to the end of the haul,
but logic prevails and you decide that the combination of the dark and
isolation are making you paranoid. The shapes of specimens and models give way
to horrors under the cloak of the night, putting you on edge. To your right, a
Guidebook precariously sits on the corner of the statue base housing the
Ankylosaurus model. It reads 12[h Edition. You hadn't realised the 11th
Edition had been released, let alone the 12th. You make a mental note to pick
up both the next time you visit for Mila's growing collection. What is such a
happy room for you normally, in this light, feels perverse. Like someone has
rebuilt an exact model of Notlaw's Dinosaur Exhibit, just for you. Just to
watch how you move around the space. This thought brings too many irrational
doubts so you choose to discard it.........
