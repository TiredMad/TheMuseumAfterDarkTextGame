You slot the batteries into the radio and it bursts to life in shots of static
that burn your ears. The pain in your head has become unbearable. "Hello?
Hello? Can anyone hear me? I'm stu-" A reply cuts you off almost immediately,
"JESUS DUDE THE THINGS TALKING, TOM COME HEAR THIS!" "Guys I don't like this,
turn that stupid thing off"

Your head swims in pain as confusion washes over you. "Wha-why-hey-hello? can
anyone hear me? I need help"

Your hands rush to cradle your head as the pain develops into a blinding heat
behind your eyes. As you fade from consciousness, through broken static you
hear the voices say something that tightens your chest in a way you didn't
know possible...

"Dude, Spirit boxes are real, I so told you this place was haunted!"
